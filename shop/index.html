Can I sell on Etsy from Egypt?

Yes, with Payoneer

Egypt is on Etsy's list. You get paid through a free Payoneer account, then withdraw to your Egyptian bank in pounds.

  • Your name, address and ID must match exactly on Etsy and Payoneer. If they don't, the shop can be put on hold.
  • Etsy keeps about 13% + $0.50 of each sale: $0.20 to list, 6.5% transaction fee, and about 6.5% + $0.30 for payment processing in Egypt. Payoneer also charges a small fee when you withdraw.
  • Some countries pay a one-time setup fee of about $15–29. Etsy shows it during signup, if it applies.
  • Sources: eligible countries · Payoneer with Etsy · Etsy fees. Check the fee table again when you sign up.

Your first 30 days

Budget: about $3 for 15 listings ($0.20 each), plus a setup fee if Etsy shows one. Canva Free is enough. Only pay for Canva Pro (about $15/month) once you have sales.

Rules that keep the shop safe

  1. Only your own designsNo Disney, Sanrio, brand logos or celebrity names. Check that every font and graphic is allowed for commercial use.
  2. Canva's licencePrintable PDFs: use only Free Canva elements. If a design uses Pro elements, sell it as a Canva template link, never as a PDF or PNG.
  3. Your real detailsYour own name and ID on Etsy and Payoneer. No VPN and no one else's account: both can get the shop closed.
  4. Legal templates"For educational use, not legal advice". Don't claim they are valid under one country's law.
  5. Keep a sales recordOnline income is taxable in Egypt. Write down every sale from day one, and ask an accountant once sales are regular.
  6. Two channelsEtsy plus Instagram, so one platform never holds all your income.

The plan in one line

Selling to the US, from Egypt, legally

Printable coloring books for US buyers on Etsy. Money goes Etsy → Payoneer → your own Egyptian bank.

  • Etsy shows your listings to US buyers anyway. You write in US English, price in dollars and give US Letter files. You don't need a US shop or a US company.
  • Etsy keeps about $1.30 of a $5.99 sale. You keep about $4.70.
  • Etsy collects US sales tax for you.

Set it up the legal way

  1. Tax cardRegister with the Egyptian Tax Authority and choose the simplified regime (Law 6/2025): a small tax on what you sell. One visit with an accountant.
  2. Egyptian bank accountUSD + EGP account in your name (NBE, Banque Misr or CIB).
  3. PayoneerSame name, ID and address as the bank. Link the bank to it.
  4. Etsy shopShop country: Egypt. Connect Payoneer. Every detail must match your ID exactly.
  5. W-8BEN formInside Etsy's tax settings, with your Egyptian tax number. It tells the US you are not a US person.
  6. Keep a recordEvery sale and fee, every month. Tax return by April 1.

What about Nsave?

Not needed

Nsave gives you US account numbers, but the account is held by a UK e-money company. It is not a US bank in your name, and your money isn't insured.

  • On Etsy, your bank's country becomes your shop's country. A US account while you live in Egypt would mean claiming to be in the US, and the shop can be frozen.
  • Payoneer to an Egyptian bank is the clearly legal path, and it works.
  • Maybe later, as an extra dollar wallet, but only after an Egyptian accountant says it's fine.

Rules that keep the shop safe

  1. No famous charactersNo Disney, Bluey, Bobbie Goods, Sanrio or brand names in pictures, titles or tags.
  2. Say it's made with AIEtsy: choose "Designed by" (not "Handmade") and add one line in the description. Amazon: tick "AI-generated".
  3. Only clean pagesReject any page with weird hands, grey shading, random letters, or lines that don't close.
  4. Your real detailsYour own name, ID and Egyptian bank everywhere. No VPN and no one else's account.
  5. Personal-use licenseBuyers can print for themselves, not resell. It is already in every book.
  6. Keep a sales recordEvery month: sales, fees, Payoneer withdrawals. For the tax return.
